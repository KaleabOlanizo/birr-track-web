Birr Track Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026
App: Birr Track (Android), package com.kaleabolani.birrtrack
Developer: Kaleab Olani, Ethiopia
Contact: kaleabolani@gmail.com

This privacy policy explains what information Birr Track uses, why it uses it, and where that information goes. In short: Birr Track reads the SMS messages your banks and mobile-money wallets send you, turns them into a record of your spending and income, and keeps everything on your phone. The app has no internet access, so it cannot send your data anywhere.

1. What the app does

Birr Track is a personal expense and income tracker for Ethiopian banks and mobile-money wallets (for example CBE, telebirr, Bank of Abyssinia, Awash Bank, Dashen Bank, CBE Birr and M-PESA). You choose which SMS senders to track. When one of those senders sends you a transaction SMS, the app reads it and records the debit or credit, amount, fee, balance and payee, then puts the transaction into a category. It also shows balances, budgets and spending charts.

Birr Track is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any bank or mobile-money provider. It cannot move money, make payments or access your bank accounts. It only reads the messages already on your phone.

2. What data the app accesses

SMS messages (permissions: READ_SMS and RECEIVE_SMS).

  • Choosing senders. When you set up tracking, the app reads your SMS inbox to show you a list of senders, how many messages each has sent, and each sender's most recent message, so you can pick the right one. This list is shown on screen only. It is not saved.
  • Tracked senders. After you choose a sender, the app saves messages from that sender only. If you ask it to, it also imports that sender's past messages.
  • New messages. When a new SMS arrives, the app checks the sender. Messages from senders you have not chosen are ignored and not saved. Messages from senders you track are read and recorded. Promotional messages and one-time passwords (OTPs) from tracked senders are kept on your phone marked as "skipped": you can view them, but they are not counted as transactions.

Notifications (permission: POST_NOTIFICATIONS). The app shows notifications on your phone: a "What was this for?" prompt after a new payment so you can pick a category, and budget alerts at 80% and 100% of a budget. Notifications are created on your phone. Nothing is sent to us.

Information you enter. Account names, categories, budgets, notes and message formats you teach the app are stored with your transactions.

The app does not access your contacts, location, call log, photos, files, camera, microphone, or any account or sign-in. It does not use advertising IDs.

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to provide the app's features on your phone:

  • recording transactions and balances from your bank and wallet SMS;
  • categorizing spending and detecting transfers between your own accounts;
  • budgets, alerts and analytics (charts, top payees, spending by category and time).

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, credit scoring, lending decisions or any purpose other than showing it to you.

4. Where the data is stored

All data is stored on your device, in the app's private database, which other apps cannot read. We do not have servers, and we never receive a copy of your data.

Device backup. Android has its own device backup feature. If you have turned on backup in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's data in your device backup (for example, to your Google account). That backup is made and stored by Android and Google under your account and settings, not by Birr Track, and we cannot access it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

5. No collection, sharing or selling

Birr Track does not collect, share, sell, rent or transfer your personal data to anyone, including us, advertisers, data brokers or other third parties. The app contains no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting services and no third-party tracking code.

6. No internet access

Birr Track does not request Android's internet permission. Because of this, the app is technically unable to send any data off your phone. You can confirm this on the app's page in Google Play ("App permissions") or in your phone's app settings.

7. Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it.

  • In the app, you can delete single transactions and budgets, and "Stop tracking" an account, which deletes its transactions and learned formats. Your SMS messages themselves are never changed or deleted by the app.
  • You can delete all app data at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Birr Track → Storage → Clear storage.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your phone (except any copy in a device backup you have enabled, which you manage in your Android or Google account settings).

Because we never receive your data, we have nothing to delete on our side. You can also revoke the SMS or notification permission at any time in your phone's settings. Without SMS permission, the app cannot record new transactions.

8. Children

Birr Track is meant for adults managing their own money. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly process children's data. Because all data stays on the device, we hold no data that we could identify or delete.

9. Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. The app has no internet access, so there is no transmission to protect or server to breach. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app and see your transactions, so we recommend using a screen lock.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top. If a change affects how your data is handled (for example, if a future version ever needed internet access), we will explain it in the app and ask for your consent before it takes effect.
