How it works

Set it up once. After that, every transaction SMS is recorded the moment it arrives, even when the app is closed.

Pick your senders

Choose your banks and wallets (CBE, telebirr, Bank of Abyssinia…) from the senders in your inbox.

Optionally import history

Bring in the messages you already have, or start tracking from today.

Everything is recorded

Amount, fees, balance, payee and category are read from each SMS, in English or Amharic.

Private by design

Your financial messages are some of the most personal data on your phone. Birr Track is built so they never leave it.

  • No internet access. The app doesn't even request Android's internet permission, so it can't upload anything.
  • No accounts, ads or tracking. Nothing is collected, shared or sold.
  • Only the senders you choose. Messages from anyone else are ignored.
  • Never sends SMS and can't move money or touch your bank accounts.
  • You're in control. Stop tracking an account or uninstall the app to delete its data.

Questions

Why does it need SMS permission?

Banks and wallets send an SMS for every transaction. Reading those messages is the only way the app can see your transactions without your bank password. It reads your inbox once to list senders, then only the senders you choose.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The app has no internet permission, so it physically can't send data. Everything is stored in the app's private storage on your phone.

Does it work with Amharic messages?

Yes. It understands English and Amharic transaction messages, including amounts in ብር, balances (ቀሪ ሒሳብ) and fees.

What if my bank's message isn't recognised?

It goes to a “Needs review” list. Tap the amount and balance in the message once, and Birr Track remembers that format for that sender.

Does it cost anything?

No. Birr Track is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
