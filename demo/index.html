See Birr Track in action

A 100-second walkthrough on a test phone with sample bank messages. No real personal data is shown.

What the video shows

  1. Before asking for any permission, the app explains what it reads, why, and that nothing leaves the phone (it has no internet permission).
  2. The user agrees and grants SMS and notification access.
  3. The user picks which sender to track (telebirr). Other senders, like promotions, are ignored.
  4. The app imports that sender's past message and turns it into a transaction: amount, fee, balance and payee.
  5. A new bank SMS arrives. It's recorded instantly and a notification asks what it was for; one tap sets the category.
  6. The overview and analysis pages show balances and spending.
